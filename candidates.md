## Seeds to beat (yours must be better, so they are listed, not copied)

- promptfade — the blankness that arrives the instant the chat box opens and the brilliant question you had evaporates.
- botpause — the half-second of hesitation before you realise the message you just read was written by a machine.
- scrollnesia — the instant forgetting of everything you read in the last ten seconds of a feed.

## The fifteen candidates (as drafted)

1. typehush — the pause after someone's typing indicator stops and no message follows.
2. draftlimbo — the typed messages you left abandoned in drafts, a place their fate is unclear. ★ winner
3. senddoubt — the brief flash of regret right after you hit send, while the words still look right.
4. unreadtide — the rising pile of things you mean to read that keeps coming.
5. pingdrift — the pings that arrive while you're working and drift past unread.
6. tablag — the tabs you meant to close and never quite get to, staying open past your patience.
7. readdrift — the drifting of your attention from what you're reading on a feed beside you.
8. botglance — the impression a machine-written message gives you before you notice it was from a bot.
9. tapvoid — a tap that lands on nothing, where the link is gone or the app is already open.
10. alertidle — the alert that lands while you're busy and sits unread for hours.
11. pingtread — the stepping in place while waiting for the reply to the last ping.
12. mutehush — the silent reading of notifications you've muted, because they keep coming.
13. tabidle — the idle open tabs that gather while you work.
14. bouncewait — the waiting that bounces off your thoughts; you keep checking but nothing new.
15. feedlull — the moment the feed stops delivering and your attention rests, unclaimed.

## Shortlist (top three)

1. draftlimbo — draft + limbo: the feeling of typed messages that sit, fate unclear.
2. typehush — the anticlimax beat after the typing indicator ends with no words following.
3. unreadtide — the rising tide of things you mean to read.

## Why draftlimbo won

Criterion 1 (new word + one clear line): all fifteen coin new English compounds, so this tier ties.
Criterion 2 (real gap, recognised at once): draftlimbo is the one a reader recognises most instantly — everyone has typed messages they left abandoned in drafts; the word hands them the feeling in one clause.
Criterion 3 (definition exact, self-contained): "the typed messages you left abandoned in drafts, a place their fate is unclear." — one line, no second sentence, no example.
Criterion 4 (well formed, sayable, spellable, memorable): draft-lim-bo, ten letters, blends like doomscrolling/hangry.
Criterion 5 (fresh pairing): "draft" is everyday writing; the blend with "limbo" gives the sense of being stuck-in-draft.

## Winner entry (chosen by main.py)

draftlimbo — the typed messages you left abandoned in drafts, a place their fate is unclear.
